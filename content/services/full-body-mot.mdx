Think of it like a service for your body. The Full Body MOT uses the same SOAP framework clinicians use to assess and plan treatment, applied to your whole body rather than one sore spot.

## Why get a Full Body MOT?

Most injuries don't appear out of nowhere. They build up from stiffness, weakness or compensation patterns you might not notice until something gives. An MOT finds those weak links early.

It's ideal if you're:

- starting a marathon or half-marathon block
- returning to sport after a long break
- getting recurring niggles in different places
- simply wanting to know where you stand and what to work on

## What you get

A clear, prioritised picture of how your body is moving, treatment for anything that needs it on the day, and a plan that tells you exactly what to focus on.
