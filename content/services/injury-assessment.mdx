Whether it's a knee that flares up every time your mileage climbs, a hamstring you tweaked at five-a-side, or a shoulder that's been "not quite right" for months, the first step is the same: find out exactly what's going on.

## Understand it, then fix it

An injury assessment is about more than where it hurts. We'll look at *why* it happened (training load, movement patterns, strength deficits, old injuries) so the plan fixes the cause, not just the symptoms.

You'll get treatment in the same session where it's appropriate, and you'll leave with a rehab programme you understand and can actually stick to.

## For runners and athletes

Based at Made Running Hub, I see a lot of running injuries: runner's knee, shin splints, Achilles and calf problems, plantar heel pain, hip and glute issues. I also treat footballers, rugby players, lifters and anyone whose body is stopping them doing what they love.
