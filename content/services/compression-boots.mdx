Whether you're an athlete, a gym-goer or just need some restorative self-care, compression boots give your legs the recovery they deserve.

## How it works

The boots use dynamic air compression: chambers inflate and release in sequence from your feet upwards, gently squeezing the muscles in your legs. This helps move fluid out of tired tissue, supports circulation and leaves your legs feeling lighter.

## Two session lengths

- **Standard**: a quick recovery boost after a workout or a long day
- **Extended**: deeper recovery and relaxation after races, long runs or heavy blocks

## Is it safe for me?

Compression therapy isn't suitable if you have, or have had, a DVT or blood clot, a circulatory or vascular condition, heart failure, open wounds on your legs, or if you're pregnant. You'll answer a short safety check when you book.
