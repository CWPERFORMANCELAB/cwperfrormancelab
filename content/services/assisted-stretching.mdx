Stretching on your own only gets you so far. With assisted stretching, I guide you through personalised stretches, controlling the position and the pressure so your muscles can relax and lengthen safely.

## Who it's for

- Athletes and gym-goers wanting better range for squats, sprints or lifts
- Runners with tight hips, hamstrings and calves
- Anyone who sits a lot and feels stiff by the end of the day

## Why it works

Because you're fully supported, your muscles can let go rather than guarding. Using techniques like contract–relax (PNF), we can safely unlock more range than you'd reach alone, and you'll feel the difference by the time you stand up.
