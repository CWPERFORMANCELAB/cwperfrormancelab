Your legs have just carried you 26.2 miles, or through a 60-mile training week. Sports massage helps them recover.

## Built for recovery

Sports massage is deep tissue work that releases muscle tension, eases knots and helps prevent injuries. Every session is tailored to you, using a mix of techniques:

- **Effleurage**: long, smooth strokes to warm the tissue and increase blood flow
- **Petrissage**: kneading to release tight muscle and support the lymphatic system
- **Tapotement**: rhythmic percussion to stimulate local circulation

Together they loosen fascia, ease tight muscles and leave you feeling looser and lighter.

## Post-marathon recovery

Manchester Marathon, a spring half or your first 10K: a sports massage in the days after a race helps reduce soreness and get you moving comfortably again. It's just as valuable in the build-up, to keep you training consistently.

## Not just for runners

Lifters, footballers, cyclists, desk-bound backs: anyone with tight, overworked muscles will feel the difference.
