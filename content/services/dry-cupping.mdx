Dry cupping is a traditional technique with a modern following among athletes. Cups create suction on the skin, lifting the tissue underneath, which can help ease tension, relieve pain and improve blood flow to the area.

## Is it safe for me?

For most people, yes. But cupping isn't suitable if you have a bleeding disorder or take blood thinners, are pregnant, have broken or irritated skin in the area, or have a history of blood clots. You'll answer a short safety check when you book, and if anything flags up, I'll get in touch to talk it through first.

## About the marks

Cupping often leaves circular marks. They're not bruises from impact and they're not painful. They usually fade within a few days to a week.
