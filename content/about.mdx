I'm Christopher, a Sport Rehabilitator based at Made Running Hub in Manchester.

## Why I do this

Growing up, I watched my dad live with arthritis. It was hard seeing someone I love struggle, and harder still wanting to help without knowing how. That feeling stayed with me.

Then I tore my medial meniscus playing rugby. It was a tough time, physically and mentally. Rehabilitating myself back to sport taught me how much the right programme, clear education and proper support matter. When you understand *why* something hurts and *what* each exercise is for, recovery stops being guesswork.

I got back to sport, and that experience is the reason I care so much about education, proper rehab and sound return-to-sport protocols.

## My background

I played football all through school before moving into rugby, so I know first-hand what sport asks of your body. I hold a BSc (Hons) in Sport Rehabilitation from the University of Salford.

## What I'm here to do

My mission is simple: to help you move better, perform better and get back to the things you love with confidence.

That means evidence-based rehabilitation, performance training and education, and it means looking beyond the injury itself. Together we'll work out why it happened, how to stop it coming back, and how to build you back stronger than before.

Whether you're an athlete coming back from injury, a runner chasing a PB, or someone who just wants to move and feel better day to day, you'll get personalised guidance, honest advice and a plan that fits your life.

## Part of the running community

I'm based at Made Running Hub, so I spend my days around runners, from first-timers training for a 5K to club athletes building towards a marathon. If you run, I speak your language: mileage, niggles, taper weeks and race-day nerves included.
