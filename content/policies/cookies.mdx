We keep cookies to a minimum.

## Essential cookies (always on)

These make the site work and can't be switched off:

| Cookie | Purpose | Lasts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cw_consent` | Remembers your cookie choice | 12 months |
| `cw_admin` | Keeps the practitioner signed in to the dashboard | 12 hours |
| Stripe cookies | Fraud prevention during secure checkout (set on Stripe's pages) | Varies |

## Optional cookies (only with your consent)

If you choose "Accept all", we may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages are useful. No optional cookies are set until you agree, and you can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

## Embedded maps

The map on our contact page is provided by Google Maps and only loads when you choose to view it.
