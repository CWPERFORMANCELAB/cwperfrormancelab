## What we collect

- **Contact details**: your name, email address, phone number and date of birth.
- **Emergency contact**: a name and phone number.
- **Health information**: details of your injury or complaint, relevant medical history, medications, safety screening answers and consent forms. This is *special category data* under UK GDPR.
- **Activity information**: your sport, training level and goals.
- **Booking and payment records**: appointments, amounts paid and refunds. Card details are handled by Stripe and never stored by us.
- **Marketing preference**: whether you've opted in to occasional updates.

## Why we use it, and our lawful basis

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- |
| Booking and delivering your treatment | Contract |
| Assessing and treating you safely (health data) | Provision of health care (UK GDPR Art. 9(2)(h)), plus your explicit consent |
| Appointment confirmations and reminders | Contract / legitimate interests |
| Keeping clinical records | Legal obligation / legitimate interests |
| Marketing emails | Your consent (you can withdraw at any time) |

## How it's protected

Your data is stored in the UK/EU, encrypted in transit and at rest. Health information is additionally encrypted at field level. Access is limited to your practitioner, protected by two-factor authentication, and every access is logged.

## Who we share it with

Only trusted service providers that help run the clinic: our hosting and database provider, Stripe (payments), our email and SMS providers, and Google Calendar (appointment times only, never health details) if connected. We never sell your data.
